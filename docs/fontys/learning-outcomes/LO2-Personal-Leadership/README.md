## Reflection

The project showcased strong personal leadership through self-directed learning and proactive initiative. Setting clear learning goals at the start provided direction, while regular feedback sessions with supervisors ensured alignment and continuous improvement. The most significant demonstration of leadership was independently researching and deploying to production Kubernetes, which required extensive self-directed learning.

Challenges included time estimation accuracy, balancing multiple learning objectives, and managing the entire project independently. However, these challenges fostered resilience and problem-solving skills. The successful production deployment and frontend redesign initiatives demonstrated ability to take ownership and drive complex technical projects.

Key lessons learned include the importance of proactive communication, the value of structured reflection, and the effectiveness of self-directed learning when combined with regular feedback. Future development will focus on improving time estimation, practicing delegation in team settings, and enhancing technical presentation capabilities.
